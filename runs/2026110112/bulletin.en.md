- It has been named Cyclone Murasu (JTWC 04B, 50 knots at 1 Nov 17:30 IST).
- It is likely to intensify into a severe cyclonic storm over the westcentral Bay of Bengal by the night of 1 Nov.
- It is likely to move northwestwards.
- It is likely to cross the Andhra Pradesh coast near Machilipatnam around the morning of 3 Nov as a severe cyclonic storm, with lowest central pressure of about 997 hPa and maximum winds near 65 knots.
- After landfall it is likely to weaken into a well-marked low pressure area over Telangana by the morning of 4 Nov.
- A western disturbance is likely to bring rain or snow to the western Himalayan region on 1 Nov, 3 Nov and 4 Nov.
